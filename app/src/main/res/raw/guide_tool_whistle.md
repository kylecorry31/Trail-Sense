Use the Whistle tool to signal for help.

**Note:** Turn your volume up to the maximum, or connect to an external speaker. As with any tool in Trail Sense, it is always best to carry a real whistle with you.

## Play a whistle sound
Tap and hold the whistle button to play a whistle sound.

## Play the SOS signal
Tap the SOS button to play the SOS distress signal (3 short, 3 long, 3 short) continuously.

## Play the help signal
Tap the help button to play the help distress signal (3 long) continuously.

## Play other signals
To play common signals, long-press the help button. The signals are:

- **Where are you** and **Acknowledged**: 1 long
- **Come here**: 2 long

## Quick action
To use the Whistle quick action, enable it in the settings for the tab you want it on. Tap and hold the quick action button to play a whistle sound.

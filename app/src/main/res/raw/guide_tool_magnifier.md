Use the Magnifier tool to magnify close-up objects and text with your phone's camera.

## Zoom in or out
To change the magnification, do one of the following:

- Drag the zoom slider at the bottom of the screen.
- Pinch with two fingers on the camera preview.

## Change the focus mode
To switch between focus modes, tap the focus button in the bottom-left corner of the controls. The modes are:

- **Auto focus**: The button shows a focus icon. The camera focuses automatically. Use this for objects at varying distances.
- **Close-up focus**: The button shows a magnifier icon. The focus is locked for nearby objects. Use this to read text or inspect fine details.

## Turn on the torch
To turn on the flashlight, tap the torch button in the top-right corner.

## Freeze the image
To pause the camera on the current frame, tap the freeze button in the bottom-right corner of the controls. This lets you examine the image without holding the phone still. While the image is frozen, the other controls are hidden. To return to the live camera view, tap the play button in the center of the controls.

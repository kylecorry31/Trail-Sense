Use the Map tool to see your beacons, paths, offline maps, and other map layers in a single place.

**Warning:** Always carry a physical map as a backup, and check Trail Sense's accuracy against it. This tool isn't a replacement for a dedicated maps app, and it shows only the maps you have loaded into the Offline Maps tool.

## Move around the map
You can move around the map in the following ways:

- **Pan**: Drag the map.
- **Zoom**: Pinch the map, or tap the zoom buttons in the bottom-right corner.
- **Center on your location**: Tap the GPS button in the bottom-right corner.

The GPS button has three states. Each tap moves to the next state:

1. The map is centered on your location.
2. The map is locked to your location and orientation.
3. The map is unlocked.

## Change the map time
If a time-dependent layer, such as tides or solar eclipse, is enabled, a clock button appears in the top-right corner. To change the time used by these layers, do the following:

1. Tap the clock button.
2. Drag the slider to change the time, or tap the time text to enter a specific time.
3. Use the date picker and arrows to select the desired date.

The map updates to show the selected time. To reset to the current time, tap the clock button again.

## Navigate to a beacon
To start navigation from the map, do one of the following:

- Tap a beacon.
- Long-press a point on the map, and select **Navigate**.

While you navigate, the bottom of the screen shows the distance, direction, and estimated time of arrival (ETA). To open the beacon, tap its name in the navigation sheet. To cancel navigation, tap the **X** button.

## Create a beacon
To create a beacon from the map, do the following:

1. Long-press a point on the map.
2. Select **Beacon**.

The **Create Beacon** screen opens with the location filled in. For more information about beacons, see the Beacons guide.

## Measure a distance
To measure the distance between points, do the following:

1. Tap the menu button in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Measure** or **Create path**.
3. Tap the map to place points.

The total distance is shown at the bottom of the screen. The start of the path is shown as a dot and the end is shown as a flag. To cancel, tap the **X** button in the bottom-right corner.

To measure the distance from your location to a point, long-press the point on the map, and tap **Distance**. The long-press menu also shows the distance, direction, and elevation difference from your location to the point.

## Edit the points of a measurement
After you place points, you can edit them in the following ways:

- **Move a point**: Drag the point. While you drag, a magnified view of the area under your finger appears in the top corner of the map on the opposite side.
- **Return to a point**: Tap the point to add a new point at the same location. Use this to close a loop or to retrace a section.
- **Select a point**: Long-press the point. New points are added after the selected point, or before it if you select the start of the path. To deselect the point, long-press it again.
- **Undo or redo**: Tap the undo or redo button at the bottom of the screen.

When no point is selected, new points are added to the end of the path.

## Create a path
To convert the points you placed into a saved path, tap **Create path** at the bottom of the screen. For the steps to place points, see the Measure a distance section.

## Check the elevation of a location
To see the elevation of a location from the DEM, long-press the location on the map.

## Use layers
Layers display information on the map. For more information, see the Map Layers guide.

## Trace a map
To trace a map onto paper, do the following:

1. Tap the menu button in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Trace**.
3. Move into a shady area, or block out the sun with a cloth.
4. Place paper over your screen so you can see the map through the paper.
5. Trace the map with a pen or pencil. Avoid markers or pens that can bleed through the paper, and be careful not to slide the paper.
6. When you finish, tap the lock icon in the bottom-right corner to turn off trace mode.

The bottom navigation is disabled until you turn off trace mode.

## Check the sensor status
The GPS and compass accuracy are shown as status badges in the bottom-left corner of the map. To see tips for improving the location and compass accuracy, tap a badge.

## Map settings
The following settings are in **Settings > Map**:

- **Show on lock screen while open**: Keeps the Map tool visible over your lock screen while it is open.
- **Remember map state**: Restores the map position, zoom, and GPS lock mode the next time you open the Map tool.
- **High detail mode**: Loads more tiles for higher quality. Use this if the tiles look blurry. It uses more memory and loads more slowly.

## Widgets
You can place the following widgets on your device's home screen or view them in the app:

- **Map**: Shows your map layers centered on your current location, always oriented north-up. The widget updates automatically. Tap it to open the Map tool.

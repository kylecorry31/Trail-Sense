Use the Tides tool to keep track of the tides at a specific location.

**Warning:** Tide predictions are estimates, and you shouldn't rely on them exclusively. Whenever possible, use an official tide table, and enter its data into Trail Sense for accuracy.

## Create a tide table
Before you go to a coastal area, find an official tide table for accurate times and water heights. See the Find official tide tables section. Then enter it in Trail Sense:

1. Tap the tides button in the top-right corner of the screen.
2. Tap the **+** button in the bottom-right corner.
3. Optional: Enter a name and location for the tide.
4. Set the tide frequency, which is the number of high tides that usually occur in a day. Most places have 2, which is called semidiurnal.
5. Scroll down to the tide table section.
6. Tap **H** for high tide or **L** for low tide to switch between them.
7. Tap **Time not set (required)**, select the date, tap **OK**, select the time, and tap **OK**.
8. Optional: Tap the **-** in the **Height** column, and enter the height of the tide.
9. To add another tide, tap the **+** button in the top-left corner of the table.
10. Optional: To choose how Trail Sense estimates tides, tap the **Estimate method** dropdown. See the Estimation methods section.
11. Tap the checkmark in the top-right corner to save the tide table. The checkmark appears after you set the time of at least one tide.

To delete a tide from the table, tap the **X** icon on the right side of the tide.

For the best accuracy, enter all the tides for each relevant day. The tide chart shows which tides you entered and which are estimated.

### Estimation methods
Trail Sense can estimate tides with the following methods:

- **Tide clock**: Assumes a fixed tidal cycle. It requires at least one known tide.
- **Lunitidal interval (auto)**: Uses the moon's position to estimate tides. It might be more accurate in some locations. It calculates the lunitidal interval automatically and requires at least one known tide.
- **Lunitidal interval (local)**: Uses the moon's position and a lunitidal interval that you enter in hours and minutes for your location. It requires a location but not a tide table.
- **Lunitidal interval (UTC/Greenwich)**: Uses the moon's position and a lunitidal interval that you enter in hours and minutes for UTC/Greenwich. It requires neither a location nor a tide table.
- **Tide model (auto)**: Estimates the tide near the location you entered or, if you didn't enter one, near you. For most locations, the tides are accurate to within an hour.

## View a tide table
By default, the Tides tool shows the tide closest to your location when you open it. To show the last tide you viewed instead, change **Settings > Tides > Show closest tide**.

To view a different tide, do the following:

1. Tap the tides button in the top-right corner of the screen. A list of the tide tables you created opens.
2. Tap the tide.

### View the nearby tide
The nearby tide shows the tide near your location from an offline tide model. For most locations, the tide is accurate to within an hour, but use official tide tables whenever possible. The model might be inaccurate in some areas, especially areas with complicated geography.

If you aren't close to a tide you entered, the nearby tide is shown by default. You can't edit or delete the nearby tide table.

## Read the tide chart
The tide chart shows the tides for the selected day. The X-axis is time, and the Y-axis is tide height. A dot marks the current time. The screen has the following parts:

- **Top of the screen**: The current tide level and, if available, the tide height. A down arrow means the tide is falling, and an up arrow means the tide is rising.
- **Bottom of the screen**: A tide table for the selected day. It shows the tide height if you entered it. Otherwise, the height is estimated from the tides you entered. Estimated tides might not be entirely accurate.

Today is selected by default. To select another day, do one of the following:

- Tap the calendar icon at the top of the screen.
- Tap the arrows to move to the next or previous date.
- Long-press the calendar icon to jump back to today.

## Edit a tide table
To edit a tide table, do the following:

1. Tap the tides button in the top-right corner of the screen.
2. Tap the menu button on the right side of the tide you want to edit.
3. Select **Edit**.
4. Edit the table on the screen that opens. It is the same screen you use to create a tide table.
5. Tap the checkmark in the top-right corner to save your changes.

## Delete a tide table
To delete a tide table, do the following:

1. Tap the tides button in the top-right corner of the screen.
2. Tap the menu button on the right side of the tide you want to delete.
3. Select **Delete**.

## Show or hide a tide on the map
By default, tides that have a location appear on the Tides map layer. To hide or show a tide, do the following:

1. Tap the tides button in the top-right corner of the screen.
2. Tap the menu button on the right side of the tide you want to hide or show.
3. Select **Hide** or **Show**.

## Estimate tides without a tide table
If you don't have a tide table, you can estimate the tides with the Tides tool. This is only an estimate, so use an officially published tide table when possible.

If you know whether the tide is currently high or low, do the following:

1. Create a tide table with a single entry that uses the current time and tide level. See the Create a tide table section.
2. Save the tide table.

The Tides tool estimates the tidal cycle from that entry. Use this only for short-term predictions, because the accuracy decreases the farther you are from the reference tide.

## Find official tide tables
To find an official tide table, search the Internet for tides at your location. The following sites are recommended by the community.

### Worldwide
- [tides4fishing](https://tides4fishing.com/)
- [tide-forecast.com](https://www.tide-forecast.com/)
- [SHOM](https://maree.shom.fr/)

### Australia
- [Bureau of Meteorology: Tidal Predictions](https://www.bom.gov.au/australia/tides/)

### Germany
- [BSH](https://gezeiten.bsh.de/)

### Netherlands
- [Rijkswaterstaat](https://www.rijkswaterstaat.nl/water/waterdata-en-waterberichtgeving/waterdata/getij)

### UK
- [UK Hydrographic Office: EasyTide](https://easytide.admiralty.co.uk/)

### UK + Ireland
- [NTSLF Tides](https://ntslf.org/tides/predictions)

### USA
- [NOAA Tides & Currents](https://tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/tide_predictions.html)
- [Tides.net](https://tides.net/)

## Widgets
You can place the following widgets on your device's home screen or view them in the app:

- **Tides**: Shows the status of the nearest tide.
- **Tide chart**: Shows the chart of the water level of the nearest tide.

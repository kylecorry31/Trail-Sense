Use the Solar Panel Aligner tool to position a solar panel for the most power while you camp.

## Align a solar panel
To align a solar panel, do the following:

1. To choose how long you want to use the solar panel, tap **2h** to enter a custom duration, or tap **Today** to position the panel for the rest of the day.
2. Place your phone on the face of the solar panel.
3. Rotate the solar panel in the direction the arrows on the screen indicate.

For both the tilt and the azimuth, the small number on the left is the current angle, and the large number on the right is the angle to align to. A checkmark appears when the tilt or the azimuth is within 5 degrees of the target. An estimate of the power generated is shown at the bottom of the screen.

Use the Metal Detector tool to find magnetic metals, such as iron. It is useful for finding dropped keys or knives. It can't detect coins or jewelry.

## Calibrate the detector
Calibration improves the accuracy of the readings. The detector calibrates automatically when it starts. To calibrate it again, do the following:

1. Hold your phone 2-3 inches (5-8 cm) above the ground with the screen facing up.
2. Tap **Calibrate**.

Recalibrate occasionally to keep the readings accurate.

## Detect metal
To detect metal, do the following:

1. Calibrate the detector.
2. Hold your phone 2-3 inches (5-8 cm) above the area you want to scan with the screen facing up.
3. Move your phone slowly, keeping the distance and orientation consistent.

When the detector finds metal, the phone vibrates and shows a detection indicator next to the reading. To turn the vibration on or off, tap the vibration icon in the top-left corner.

## Read the screen
The screen shows the following:

- **Graph**: Shows the magnetic field strength over time. Metal causes spikes up or down. If spikes occur that don't trigger the detector, lower the threshold to increase sensitivity.
- **Readings**: The field strength in microteslas (uT), shown at the top of the screen.
- **Badge**: Shows the compass (magnetometer) accuracy. Tap it to see calibration and accuracy tips.

## Adjust the sensitivity
To adjust the sensitivity, drag the on-screen threshold slider. Lower threshold values increase sensitivity, and higher values decrease it.

### Use high sensitivity mode
High sensitivity mode removes noise filtering for the maximum sensitivity. The graph becomes noisier, and false positives occur more often.

To use it, turn on the **High sensitivity** switch.

## Turn on audio feedback
To play a tone when metal is detected, tap the speaker icon in the top-right corner. The volume increases with the field strength.

## Find the direction of metal (experimental)
This experimental feature tries to show the direction of the metal with two dots on the screen. One dot points toward the metal, and the other points away from it. It isn't possible to tell which is which. This feature requires a gyroscope sensor.

To turn it on, enable **Settings > Experimental > Show metal direction**.

To find the metal, do the following:

1. Move your phone toward one of the dots.
2. Check the number in the center of the screen. If the number increases, you are getting closer to the metal.

If the dots swap, you have likely just passed over the metal.

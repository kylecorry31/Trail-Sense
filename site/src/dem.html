Digital Elevation Model (DEM)

A digital elevation model (DEM) is an elevation map which can be used in place of GPS or barometric altitude readings for a more stable reading. The Trail Sense models are generated using ETOPO data. The larger the model, the more accurate it will be. The largest model has a horizontal resolution of 15 arc seconds, which is 1 / 240 degrees or around 400 meters. The mini model is built into the app.

Quantiles are how far off the model is at 50% and 90% of locations. For example, with a 90% quantile of ± 40m, nine out of ten locations are within 40m of the actual elevation. They are calculated by comparing the models to the Copernicus DEM (a more detailed 90 meter model) at around 330 random locations in hiking areas around the world, such as the Alps, the Himalayas, the Appalachians, and the Midwest. Your results may vary.

The water mask indicates whether a model can distinguish between land and water, which allows map layers to be rendered more accurately.

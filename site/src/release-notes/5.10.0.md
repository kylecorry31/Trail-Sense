This contains a list of the changes in Trail Sense 5.10.0.

## Augmented Reality

- Available in beta - view beacons, paths, and sun/moon with camera
- Not available on devices without a compass
- Toggle layers
- Adjustable path view distance
- Show compass / GPS accuracy

## Clinometer

- Show measurement on camera view

## Astronomy

- View sun / moon path in 3D
- Show current direction and altitude of sun / moon on click

## Metal Detector

- Play sound when metal is detected

This contains a list of the changes in Trail Sense 6.6.0.

## Widgets

- Homescreen and in-app widgets (long press bottom navigation):
    - Weather
    - Pressure
    - Pressure chart
    - Sun
    - Moon
    - Sun & moon chart
    - Elevation
    - Location
    - Tide
    - Tide chart
    - Pedometer
    - Backtrack
    - Flashlight
    - Nearby beacons

## Tides

- Worldwide tide model (offline)
- Nearby tide (displays the tide for your location)

## Backup

- Setting for automatic backup (once daily, keeps last 2 backups)

## Augmented Reality

- Show stars at night (top 50 brightest)

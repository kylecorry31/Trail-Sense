Offline Maps

Trail Maps

Trail Sense supports Mapsforge maps. Here are some places to find them:

  • OpenAndroMaps: Select your region > Download V5 Map > Karte/Map to get the .map file, includes country/state/province borders.
  • Mapsforge Download Server
  • Mirror Rechenzentrum der Hochschule Esslingen, University of Applied Sciences

Photo Maps

Trail Sense supports Geospatial PDFs. Here are some places to find them:

  • Caltopo
  • QGIS (advanced, requires installing on a computer)

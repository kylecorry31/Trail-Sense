Trail Sense

An offline multi-tool Android app for hiking, backpacking, camping, wilderness survival, foraging, and hunting.

Features

Nearby

View nearby beacons and paths on your compass.

Offline Maps

Use a photo as a map or import offline maps.

Backtrack

Trace back your steps.

Weather

Get hyperlocal weather forecasts and alerts.

Astronomy

View the positions of the sun and moon.

Tools

Flashlight, pedometer, signal finder, survival guide, field guide, packing lists and more.

Resources

User Guide

Guides for each tool and setting in the app.

Maps

Where to find Mapsforge trail maps and Geospatial PDFs.

DEM

Digital elevation models for more stable altitude readings.

Wilderness Survival Guide Book

A survival guide for hikers, backpackers, and campers, covering signaling for help, shelter, food and water, fire, and weather. Available to download or read online.

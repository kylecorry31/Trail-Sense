The Species Identification tool uses an offline iNaturalist computer vision model to suggest species visible through your phone's camera.

## Identifying a species
Center a plant, animal, or fungus inside the square frame and hold the camera steady. The best match and two alternatives update automatically. A higher percentage means the model found a closer visual match, but it does not guarantee the identification is correct.

For better results, fill the frame with one subject, use good lighting, keep the subject in focus, and try several angles. The torch can help with nearby subjects in low light.

## Offline model
Identification runs entirely on your device and does not upload camera images. Trail Sense includes iNaturalist's public small model, which covers about 500 taxa and cannot identify every species. The model and taxonomy are provided by iNaturalist under the MIT License.

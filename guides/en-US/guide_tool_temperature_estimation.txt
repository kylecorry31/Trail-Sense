The Temperature Estimation tool can be used to estimate the temperature at a different elevation, as well as how the temperature feels with wind and humidity.

## Estimating temperature
To estimate the temperature at another elevation, you need to know your current elevation and the current temperature, as well as the elevation at which you want to estimate the temperature.

To estimate the temperature:

1. Either tap 'Autofill' or enter the 'Base elevation' (your elevation) and 'Current temperature' (the temperature at your location).
2. Then enter the 'Destination elevation' (the elevation at which you want to estimate the temperature).
3. The estimated temperature will be shown at the top of the screen.

## Wind chill and heat index
You can optionally enter the 'Wind speed' to factor in wind chill, and the 'Humidity' (0 to 100) to factor in the heat index. The estimated temperature at the top of the screen will be adjusted to how the temperature feels. If you do not enter a destination elevation, the estimate is for your current elevation.

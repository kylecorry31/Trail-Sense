Use the Cliff Height tool to estimate the height of a cliff by timing a falling object.

**Warning:** You are responsible for using this tool safely. Use it only when it is safe, and do not stand too close to the edge of the cliff.

This tool is experimental and hidden by default. To turn it on, enable **Settings > Experimental > Cliff Height**.

## Estimate the height of a cliff
To estimate the height of a cliff, do the following:

1. Make sure the area below the cliff is safe, with no risk of landslides and no people or animals.
2. Hold the measure button.
3. Drop a small rock or similar object down the cliff.
4. Release the button when the object hits the bottom.

The height of the cliff is shown at the top of the screen.

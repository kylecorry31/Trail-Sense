Use the Flashlight tool to provide light or signal for help.

**Note:** As with any tool in Trail Sense, it is always best to carry a real flashlight with you.

## Turn the flashlight on or off
Tap the flashlight button to turn the flashlight on or off. While the flashlight is on in any mode, a notification lets you turn it off quickly.

On supported devices, use the slider at the bottom of the screen to adjust the brightness.

## Use the strobe
**Warning:** The strobe has flashing lights, which can be dangerous for some users. Prolonged use can drain the battery quickly or damage your camera's flash.

To use the strobe, do the following:

1. Drag the dial to the strobe frequency you want. The numbers 1 to 9 and 200 are strobe speeds, and higher numbers flash faster. **0** is a steady light.
2. The first time, read the **Flashing lights** warning, and confirm it. If you don't confirm it, the flashlight turns on as a steady light instead.

The strobe starts as soon as you select a speed on the dial. To turn it off, tap the flashlight button.

## Signal for help with SOS
To play the SOS distress signal (3 short, 3 long, 3 short) continuously, do the following:

1. Drag the dial to **SOS**.
2. The SOS starts as soon as you select it. To turn it off, tap the flashlight button.

## Use the screen as a flashlight
Use the screen as a flashlight for close-up work. To use the screen flashlight, do the following:

1. Tap the screen button.
2. Use the slider at the bottom of the screen to adjust the brightness.
3. Optional: Tap the button in the top-right corner of the screen to switch the color between red, which preserves your night vision, and white, which is brighter. The button shows the color you will switch to.

To turn off the screen flashlight, press the back button or tap **Turn off**. Trail Sense saves the brightness and color for next time.

To control the brightness of the screen flashlight with the volume buttons, enable **Settings > Flashlight > Control screen flashlight brightness with volume buttons**.

## Turn off the flashlight automatically
To save battery if you forget to turn off the flashlight, do the following:

1. Enable **Settings > Flashlight > Automatically turn off**.
2. Set **Turn off after** to the amount of time.

## Toggle the flashlight with the volume buttons
To turn the flashlight on or off when you press either volume button, enable **Settings > Flashlight > Toggle with volume buttons**. This works only while Trail Sense is open.

## Quick action
To use the following quick actions, add them to a tab, as described in the Tools guide:

- **Flashlight**: Tap the quick action button to turn the flashlight on or off. Long-press it to open the Flashlight tool.
- **Screen Flashlight**: Tap the quick action button to open the screen flashlight.

## Widgets
You can place the following widgets on your device's home screen or view them in the app:

- **Flashlight**: Turns the flashlight on or off.

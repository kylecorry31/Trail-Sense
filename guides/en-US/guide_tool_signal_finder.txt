Use the Signal Finder tool to find a place where you have cell signal.

**Warning:** The accuracy of the tower locations varies. Some towers might have been decommissioned, and some locations might be inaccurate.

**Note:** Android requires Trail Sense to have the Location permission to show nearby cell signal details.

## View the signals your phone detects
The signals your phone detects are shown at the top of the list. Each signal shows the following information:

- The network type.
- The signal strength.
- The estimated distance to the tower, if available. To refresh the distance, turn airplane mode on and off.
- The time the signal was detected.
- Whether you have full service or only emergency calls. Full service means the tower is for your provider.

## Find nearby cell towers
A list of nearby cell towers is shown at the bottom of the list. The tower locations are approximate and come from OpenCelliD, Mozilla Location Service, and FCC Antenna Registrations. They are typically within a mile or kilometer of the actual tower.

Each tower shows the following information:

- The distance from your location.
- The direction from your location.

To navigate to a tower or create a beacon for it, use the menu button on the right side of the tower.

## Place an emergency call
You can normally place an emergency call if you have cell signal from any provider.

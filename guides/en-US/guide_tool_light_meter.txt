Use the Light Meter tool to measure a flashlight's beam distance, which is how far the flashlight projects usable light.

This tool is available only on phones that have a light sensor.

## Measure a flashlight's beam distance
To measure a flashlight's beam distance, do the following:

1. Go into a dark room.
2. Place your phone on the floor with the screen facing up.
3. Enter the distance at which you will hold the flashlight from the phone, for example 3 feet (1 meter).
4. Hold the flashlight at that distance, point it at the phone screen, and turn it on.
5. Move the flashlight around, keeping the distance the same and the flashlight pointed at your phone.
6. Wait until the beam distance stops increasing.

The tool keeps the brightest reading since you entered the distance or tapped **Reset**. When the beam distance stops increasing, the tool has determined the beam distance. To test another flashlight, tap **Reset**.

## Choose a beam distance
The beam distance you need depends on how you use the flashlight:

- **Camping**: A shorter beam lets you see your immediate surroundings.
- **Hiking**: A longer beam lets you see farther ahead. Start with 300 feet (100 meters).

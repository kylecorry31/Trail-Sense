Use the Clock tool to find the current time from GPS satellites and to check how accurate your device's clock is.

## View GPS time
When you first open the tool, it shows your device's time. When your device gets a GPS fix, the clock updates automatically to show the precise GPS time. If the tool can't get a GPS signal, it keeps showing the device time.

The source of the time (**Device time** or **GPS time**) is shown under the clock. To refresh the GPS time, tap the refresh icon in the top-right corner.

After the tool acquires the GPS time, it also shows how far your device's clock is from GPS time, for example "Device clock is 2 seconds slow" or "Device clock is accurate".

## Sync your device's clock with GPS time
To sync your device's clock with GPS time, do the following:

1. Tap **Update system time**.
2. Note the time shown in the dialog.
3. Tap **OK** to open your device's time settings.
4. Enter the time you noted, and wait for the sync notification before you save.
5. When the notification arrives, save the time.

Your system time is now synced with GPS time.

## Switch to a digital clock
To show a large digital clock instead of the analog clock, disable **Analog clock** in the Clock tool's settings.

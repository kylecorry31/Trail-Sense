Use the Ruler tool to measure small objects or distances on a map.

**Note:** Most devices report their screen size correctly, but compare Trail Sense with a physical ruler to make sure it is accurate. If it isn't, calibrate the ruler. See the Calibrate the ruler section.

## Measure an object
To measure an object, do the following:

1. Place the object next to the ruler.
2. Tap the screen to mark the end of the object.

The measurement is shown at the top of the screen.

## Change the units
By default, the ruler uses the distance units in **Settings > Units**. To switch the ruler between centimeters and inches, tap the units at the top of the screen.

## Change the direction
By default, 0 is at the top of the ruler. To move 0 to the bottom, enable **Settings > Ruler > Reverse ruler direction**.

## Measure a distance on a map
To measure a distance on a map, you need to know the map scale. Choose the ruler scale that matches the way the map shows its scale:

- **Ratio**: Use this if the map shows a scale such as "Scale 1:62500".
- **Verbal**: Use this if the map shows a scale such as "1 inch = 1 mile", or if it has scale bars you can measure.

To measure a distance on a map, do the following:

1. Select the **Ratio** or **Verbal** scale.
2. Enter the scale as shown on the map.
3. Measure the distance on the map with the ruler.

For example, enter the scale as follows:

- **Ratio scale**: For 1:62500, enter 1 for **From** and 62500 for **To**.
- **Verbal scale**: For 1 inch = 1 mile, enter 1 inch for **From** and 1 mile for **To**.

The distance on the map is shown at the top of the screen.

## Calibrate the ruler
To calibrate the ruler, do the following:

1. Open **Settings > Ruler > Ruler scale**.
2. Set the value to 1, and tap **OK**.
3. Open the Ruler tool, and measure 1 inch or 1 centimeter on a physical ruler.
4. Open **Settings > Ruler > Ruler scale**.
5. Enter the value Trail Sense measured for 1 inch or 1 centimeter, and tap **OK**.

For example, if Trail Sense reports 1.5 cm when you measure 1 cm on the physical ruler, enter 1.5 as the ruler scale.

## Quick action
To use the Ruler quick action, enable it in the settings for the tab you want it on. Tap the quick action button to display or hide the ruler. While the ruler is displayed, you can also tap it to hide it.

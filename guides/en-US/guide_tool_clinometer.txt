Use the Clinometer tool to measure angles. You can use it to find the slope of a hill, the height of an object, or the distance to an object. You can also use it to check the avalanche risk of a slope.

The height and distance procedures in this guide use a tree as an example. You can follow the same steps for any object.

## Measure a slope
To measure a slope or any other angle, do the following:

1. Align the line in the center of the viewfinder with the top of the hill.
2. Tap anywhere on the screen to lock the angle.
3. Read the angle at the top of the screen.

To unlock the clinometer, tap the screen again. The slope percentage (grade) is shown under the angle, and the angle is also shown on the camera viewfinder.

To lock the angle with the volume buttons instead of tapping the screen, enable **Settings > Clinometer > Lock with volume buttons**.

## Measure a slope with the side of your phone
To measure a slope by sighting along the side of your phone, do the following:

1. Tap the screen icon in the top-left corner.
2. Hold your phone vertically.
3. Look down the side of your phone, where the volume and power buttons are normally located, and point it at the top of the hill.
4. Read the angle at the top of the screen.

## Check the avalanche risk
**Warning:** Trail Sense uses a general rule of thumb to determine whether a slope has avalanche potential, and your phone's sensors limit its accuracy. If you think a slope is unsafe, find an alternate route to avoid it, or take precautions.

Most avalanches occur on slopes between 30° (57%) and 45° (100%). When you measure the angle of a slope, the avalanche risk is shown at the bottom of the screen.

The color bands on the clinometer dial also show the risk:

- **Red**: High risk
- **Yellow**: Moderate risk
- **Green**: Low risk

## Estimate the height of a tree
You can measure the height of a tree if you know the distance to it. To estimate the height of a tree, do the following:

1. Tap the ruler icon in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Height**, and tap **OK**.
3. Enter the distance to the tree, and tap **OK**.
4. Read the instructions, and tap **OK**.
5. Align the clinometer with the base of the tree.
6. Tap and hold the screen.
7. Realign the clinometer with the top of the tree.
8. Release the screen to lock the measurement.

The height of the tree is shown on the screen.

If you usually measure trees from the same distance, set a default distance in **Settings > Clinometer > Baseline distance**.

## Estimate the distance to a tree of known height
If you know the height of a tree, you can estimate the distance to it. To estimate the distance, do the following:

1. Tap the ruler icon in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Distance**, and tap **OK**.
3. Enter the height of the tree, and tap **OK**.
4. Read the instructions, and tap **OK**.
5. Align the clinometer with the base of the tree.
6. Tap and hold the screen.
7. Realign the clinometer with the top of the tree.
8. Release the screen to lock the measurement.

The distance to the tree is shown on the screen.

## Estimate the distance to a tree of unknown height
If you don't know the height of a tree, you can use your own height as a reference. To estimate the distance, do the following:

1. Stand next to the tree, and note where the top of your head is on the tree.
2. Walk away from the tree to the place where you want to measure the distance.
3. Tap the ruler icon in the top-right corner.
4. Select **Distance**, and tap **OK**.
5. Enter your height, and tap **OK**.
6. Read the instructions, and tap **OK**.
7. Align the clinometer with the base of the tree.
8. Tap and hold the screen.
9. Realign the clinometer with the place on the tree where the top of your head was.
10. Release the screen to lock the measurement.

The distance to the tree is shown on the screen. If you are looking to calculate the height of the tree, use the method described in the previous section with the distance you just measured.

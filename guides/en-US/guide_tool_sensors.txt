Use the Sensors tool to test the sensors on your device.

## View the sensors Trail Sense uses
The sensor list shows the sensors on your device that Trail Sense uses. If the accuracy of a sensor is available, it is shown on the left, and the value of the sensor is shown to the right of it.

## View all sensors
To see all of the sensors on your device, including the ones Trail Sense doesn't use, tap the button in the top-right corner of the screen.

## Quick action
To use the Recalibrate Altimeter quick action, enable it in the settings for the tab you want it on.

Tap the **Recalibrate Altimeter** button. What happens depends on your altimeter mode:

- **Barometer or Manual**: A dialog opens where you can enter your current elevation. When you confirm, the altimeter calibration is updated.
- **GPS + Barometer or DEM + Barometer**: Trail Sense reads your GPS and barometer immediately to recalibrate the altimeter baseline. A loading indicator is shown while the recalibration is in progress.
- **GPS or DEM**: Opens the altimeter calibration settings.

To open the altimeter calibration settings in any mode, long-press the quick action.

## Widgets
You can place the following widgets on your device's home screen or view them in the app:

- **Elevation**: Shows your current elevation.
- **Location**: Shows your current location.

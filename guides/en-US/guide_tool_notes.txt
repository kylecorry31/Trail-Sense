Use the Notes tool to write down notes that you can access offline.

## Create or edit a note
To create or edit a note, do the following:

1. Tap the **+** button in the bottom-right corner to create a note, or tap an existing note in the list.
2. Edit the title, the content, or both.
3. Tap the checkmark button in the bottom-right corner to save.

## Delete a note
To delete a note, do the following:

1. Tap the menu button on the right side of the note in the list.
2. Tap **Delete**.
3. Tap **OK** to confirm.

## Share a note as a QR code
**Note:** The QR code can transfer only the first 1000 characters of a note.

To share a note as a QR code, do the following:

1. Tap the menu button on the right side of the note in the list.
2. Tap **QR Code**.
3. Scan the QR code with another device to import the note.

## Quick action
To use the Note quick action, add it to a tab, as described in the Tools guide. Tap the quick action button to create a new note.

Use the Bubble Level tool to check whether a surface is flat, for example when you set up a tent or a cooking stove.

## Check if a surface is flat
To check if a surface is flat, do the following:

1. Place your device flat on the surface.
2. Wait for the bubble to settle.
3. Read the position of the bubble.

The position of the bubble shows how the surface is tilted:

- If the bubble is centered, the surface is flat.
- If the bubble is to the left, the left side of the surface is higher.
- If the bubble is at the top, the top side of the surface is higher.

When the surface is flat, the background color of the level changes. To change how close to flat the surface must be, set **Settings > Bubble Level > Level threshold**.

The angle of the surface, in degrees, is shown at the top of the screen. It includes the X axis (left/right), the Y axis (top/bottom), and the combined angle.

## Measure an angle on the side of your device
To measure an angle with the side of your device, hold the device on its side, and read the angle on the Y axis.

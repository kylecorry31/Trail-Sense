Use the Survival Guide tool to look up information about wilderness survival.

**Warning:** As with any tool in Trail Sense, this guide provides general educational information only. It is not professional training, medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it does not replace emergency services, qualified healthcare providers, local guidance, or product/manufacturer instructions. In an emergency, contact emergency services as soon as possible, follow their instructions, and do not delay seeking help to consult this guide. Do not rely on this guide, Trail Sense, or your phone as your only safety plan or means of communication. Conditions, risks, laws, and recommended practices vary by location and situation; verify information before acting and use your own judgment.

## Read a chapter
To read a chapter, do the following:

1. Open the Survival Guide tool.
2. Select the chapter you want to read.
3. Tap a section to expand its content.

## Search the survival guide
To search for content in the survival guide, do the following:

1. Enter your search in the search bar at the top of the screen.
2. Read the most relevant section, which is shown at the top of the screen.
3. Optional: Tap a search result in the list at the bottom of the screen to open its chapter with the section expanded. Each result has a brief summary.

## Quick action
To use the Survival Guide quick action, add it to a tab, as described in the Tools guide.

To use the quick action, do the following:

1. Tap the quick action button to open a search box. The most relevant section is shown.
2. Optional: Tap the section to open its chapter with the section expanded.
3. Optional: Tap the guide icon in the top-right corner of the sheet to open the Survival Guide tool with your search.
